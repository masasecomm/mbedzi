    Questions

    Is this real Google Trends data?

    Yes — TrendPulse reads the same trending panel you'd see on Google Search itself, across nine country domains, rather than a delayed export.

    How fast is "live," really?

    On the Pro and Business plans, a new query can reach your webhook or sheet in as little as one second from when it first appears. Starter syncs every 15 minutes.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Subscriptions are month-to-month with no contract — cancel from your billing portal and you'll keep access until the period ends.

    Do you store the queries you capture?

    Your captured history is kept for as long as your account is active, so you can search and export it later. You can purge it at any time.

    Can I use my own Google Sheet?

    Yes — point TrendPulse at any Sheet you control and it keeps four tabs in sync: All Captured, Rising, Dying, and Published.
